# Brag Plan: Gabay — Design A, studio-recording edit

An edit of `../design-a/` (v2) with the same story, timing, music and on-screen copy. Two changes were requested on 2026-10-09:

1. **Less zoomed-in, styled like a Screen Studio recording.** Reference: `../reference/studio-reference.png`.
   - The Mac desktop sits as a rounded window (1520×950, 16:10, radius 22) with padding on a slate gradient, with a deep shadow.
   - The camera keeps the whole desktop in view and uses gentle auto-zooms of at most 1.6× (v2 went up to 3.6×), with longer, softer eases and light motion blur.
   - The pointer is larger and leaves a click ripple on each click.
   - "It points. You click." becomes a caption pill over the recording.
   - All headline type is white on slate, with the accent in Beacon orange.
2. **Product renamed from Beside to Gabay**: in the wordmark, the page title and the share copy.

Storyboard, beat locks and audio: identical to `../design-a/brag-plan.md` (Version 2 table). Duration 31s.

The fake Preview menu-bar items (another app's UI, shown as context) are marked `data-layout-ignore`. The contrast checker was reading them where they are cropped outside the window, against the slate background.
