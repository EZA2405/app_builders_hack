## Production source

The original `build.mjs`, `make-vo.py`, `vo-lines.json` and `finish.sh` are retained beside the exports. The generator produces landscape and feed compositions; narration uses Kokoro-82M locally, and rendering uses Hyperframes 0.8.143.

Raw screen recordings, derived clips, voice-over audio and licensed music are excluded from git. The finished videos can be watched directly. Re-rendering requires the author's original production assets and local render tooling; see the credits before reusing third-party audio.

Older video drafts, reference captures and copied video-authoring skill bundles have been removed from the public tree.
