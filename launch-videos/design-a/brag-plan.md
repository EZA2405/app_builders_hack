# Brag Plan: Beside — Design A (Liquid Glass)

## What is this app?
Beside is a macOS guide that reads what is on your screen locally, highlights the exact control to click next with one plain instruction, and lets you do every click yourself — fully offline.

## The angle
A quiet, confident Apple-style product film. No hype words: the product's own calm, plain copy *is* the voice ("It points. You click.", "That opened Edit. Nothing has changed."). We watch one real task — Rosa shrinking a photo in Preview so she can email it — from asking, to a wrong turn, to done, then land the two promises that make it different: it works with Wi-Fi off, and nothing on the screen leaves the Mac.

## Version 2 (2026-10-09): Apple-style rework
User feedback on v1: "more apple-like and premium", "more motion, more fluid", "expand the screen to full screen demo". Studied two reference launch films (`launch-videos/product app video*.mp4`): word-by-word kinetic type that sharpens out of blur, 3D screens flying in, extreme macro zooms on single UI elements, motion blur on whips, depth-of-field between beats, scenes that flow into each other, lots of white space.
v1 is kept at `launch-videos/archive/design-a-v1.*`.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
White stage, kinetic type: "Stuck on your Mac?" → "Just ask." (ask in Beacon), each word sharpening out of blur.

## Key moments
- The Mac screen flies in tilted in 3D and flattens into the full frame, landing on the listening panel as the request appears word by word.
- The camera whips (motion blur) from the checklist to an extreme close-up of the Beacon ring snapping onto **Tools**, then pulls back to the card. "It points. You click."
- Wrong click on Edit → "SMALL DETOUR — That opened Edit. Nothing has changed." The camera leans into the card; the ring never moves.
- Macro on the Width field while 1200 is typed; the ring turns green with ✓.
- The finished screen floats like a product shot, then whips to the circled **Wi‑Fi Off** chip.

## Outro / punchline
"Works with Wi‑Fi off." / "Nothing on your screen leaves this Mac." → Beside mark, wordmark, "A patient guide for your Mac."

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Apple-style product launch film, kinetic type + full-screen macro UI demo
- Interpretation: continuous camera, no hard cuts; type over a softened (defocused) screen between beats; every instruction held long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
## Duration: 31s
Over the 25s /brag window on purpose: the user asked for a fuller premium launch film, and the references run 33–38s. Seven required story beats plus kinetic type need the room.
