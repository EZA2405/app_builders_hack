# Brag Plan: Beside — Design B (Island)

## What is this app?
Beside is a macOS guide that reads what is on your screen locally, highlights the exact control to click next with one plain instruction, and lets you do every click yourself — fully offline.

## The angle
A quiet, confident Apple-style product film. No hype words: the product's own calm, plain copy *is* the voice ("It points. You click.", "That opened Edit. Nothing has changed."). We watch one real task — Rosa shrinking a photo in Preview so she can email it — from asking, to a wrong turn, to done, then land the two promises that make it different: it works with Wi-Fi off, and nothing on the screen leaves the Mac.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A Mac display rises out of the dark. The black Beside island hangs from the top of the screen, already listening and the words appear as they are spoken: “Make this photo smaller so I can email it.” Headline above the Mac: **Ask out loud.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Blue corner brackets snap around **Tools** in the real menu bar with a tag “1 · Tools”; the island says “Click Tools at the top of your screen.” A cursor (the human) moves in.
- The human clicks **Edit** by mistake. Beside does not panic: “SMALL DETOUR — That opened Edit. Nothing has changed.” Brackets stay on Tools. The human clicks Tools, then Adjust Size…
- Image Dimensions sheet: brackets + number badge on Width (island shrinks to Compact so the sheet stays clear), human types 1200, brackets turn green with a ✓: “That's right. Width is 1200.” Resulting size 4.2 MB → 380 KB.

## Outro / punchline
Menu bar shows **Wi-Fi Off**. “Works with Wi-Fi off.” / “Nothing on your screen leaves this Mac.” Then the Beside mark and “A patient guide for your Mac.”

## User flow worth showing
Ask (voice) → Beside reads the screen locally → ring + one instruction → human clicks (incl. a wrong turn that Beside catches) → step confirmed → all done, offline.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Apple-style product launch film, dark keynote cut — calm, premium, restrained, the island is the hero
- Interpretation: product shot centered under a single short headline per beat, slow camera push-ins inside the display, soft crossfades, generous holds; no stat cards, no hype.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s (upper bound of the brag window; seven story beats the user required need it)

## Visual identity (from the project — design handoff, Direction B)
- Background: near-black stage `#05050A`; desktop wallpaper is the design's deep navy gradient
- Island: `#0B0B14`, Raised `#171724`, hairline `rgba(255,255,255,.08)`
- Accent: Signal `#4C8DFF` (brackets, tag, primary); key word Signal Text `#8DB6FF`; Done `#3CCB7F`; Detour chip `#FFB547`
- Text: `#F4F4F8`, Text Soft `#B4B4C4`
- Display font: SF Pro Rounded (system `ui-rounded`) Bold
- Body font: SF Pro Rounded; SF Mono 13 Semibold +4% for labels/counters only
- Strongest visual element: black island hanging from top-center + four blue corner brackets with a Signal tag

## Share copy (draft)
Beside: ask your Mac for help, out loud. It points to the exact control, you click, and it all runs on your Mac with Wi-Fi off.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, steady bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady, clean; recommended for polished)
- Music treatment: 0.30 bed, 0.6s fade in, fade out under the logo from ~23.6s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, 110 BPM. Strong cues: 13.11s (human clicks Adjust Size…), 17.47s (Wi-Fi Off headline), 22.93s (logo). Beat grid for clicks: 9.29 (wrong click on Edit), 12.02 (Tools).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music energy makes the cool Signal glow behind the display breathe. No visualizers.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched. Soft click on every human click (the human is the one acting), quiet key ticks for typing 1200, one soft impact for the offline reveal, one bell on the logo.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no sound on headline changes, nothing louder than the music swell.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Ask — 0.0–3.3s
Mac display rises in. Island opens to Full (Bb Ask): blue mic with halos, live transcript types “Make this photo smaller so I can email it.” Headline: **Ask out loud.**
Sequential/interaction: transcript appears word by word.
Audio intent: music enters gently. Audio-coupled idea: none (speech is implied, not typed).
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reading the screen — 3.3–6.6s
Island shows the Looking state (B tokens, mono label): “GOT IT — Make this photo smaller”; checklist ✓ Preview is open, ✓ Found your photo, spinner Working out the first step…; footer “Nothing on your screen leaves this Mac.” Headline: **It reads your screen.** Sub: **Right here, on this Mac.**
Sequential/interaction: checklist rows tick one after another (held).
Transition mood: soft camera move → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — It points — 6.6–9.5s
Camera pushes into the menu bar. Brackets snap to Tools + tag “1 · Tools”; island “STEP 1 / 4 · MAKING YOUR PHOTO SMALLER / Click **Tools** at the top of your screen. / Look for the blue corners, between Go and Window.” Cursor drifts in. Headline: **It points. You click.**
Sequential/interaction: cursor moves, clicks Edit at 9.29s (beat).
Transition mood: continuous → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Wrong turn — 9.5–13.3s
Edit list opens. Island shows amber chip “SMALL DETOUR” / “That opened Edit. Nothing has changed.” / “Click Tools instead. It's inside the blue corners…”. Brackets never move. Human clicks Tools (12.02s); Tools list opens, brackets hop to “Adjust Size…”, island “STEP 2 / 4 / Click Adjust Size…”; human clicks at 13.11s (strong cue). Headline: **Wrong turn? It notices.**
Transition mood: continuous → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Progress — 13.3–17.4s
Image Dimensions sheet. Island shrinks to Compact “STEP 3 / 4 / Click the **Width** box, type **1200**.”; brackets + badge 3 on Width. Human clicks, types 1200, height becomes 900, brackets turn green ✓, island “STEP 3 / 4 · DONE / That's right. Width is 1200.” Resulting size 380 KB. Headline: **Every step, checked.**
Audio-coupled idea: four key ticks for 1200; soft confirm.
Transition mood: pull back → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Offline & private — 17.4–21.4s
Camera pulls back to the whole display; Island opens to Done (Bg): “All done, Rosa.” “4.2 MB → 380 KB. Ready to attach to an email.” with four recap cards. Menu bar chip **Wi-Fi Off** gets blue brackets. Headline (17.47s beat-lock): **Works with Wi-Fi off.** Sub (18.56s): **Nothing on your screen leaves this Mac.**
Audio intent: soft impact on the headline.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Logo — 21.4–25.0s
Display recedes and fades; Beside mark (black tile, four blue corners) lands at 22.93s; “Beside” and “A patient guide for your Mac.”
Audio intent: single bell, music fades out.

**Music mood for this video:** steady, warm, premium
**Audio summary:** a clean bed rises with the Mac, human clicks are the only foreground sounds, one soft hit on offline, one bell on the mark, fade to silence.
