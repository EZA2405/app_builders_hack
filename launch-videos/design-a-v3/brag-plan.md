# Brag Plan: Gabay — Design A, v3 "Help shouldn't have to wait"

## The angle
The product exists because family tech support is exhausting: the helper is busy, and the parent is stuck (`docs/research/HANDOFF.md`, `README.md`: "their kids who are the family's 24/7 tech support"). The design already has the characters: Rosa, and her son Sam as the helper contact. The film opens on that moment and closes on its reversal.

## Visual system
- Warm paper stage `#f3eee7` with peach and periwinkle glows that breathe with the music (audio-reactive).
- Mac shown as a studio-framed recording window (1520×950, radius 22). Gentle zooms, at most 1.5×.
- One motif: the Beacon ring is the hero object. It is born, flies into the product, travels to each target, and becomes the logo.
- Human agency made visible: Rosa's pointer carries a name tag, with a ripple on every click.
- Captions are dark pills, bottom-right, clear of the progress capsule.
- Messages use iMessage-like bubbles (generic styling, no Apple branding).

## Grounding
All product UI copy is verbatim from the design handoff. The captions paraphrase README/design claims ("You click; it never clicks for you", "Never red, never 'wrong'" → small detours, "works fully offline", "Nothing on your screen leaves this Mac"). The messages are narrative framing using the design's fictional persona (Rosa) and helper contact (Sam). No accuracy numbers are shown.
